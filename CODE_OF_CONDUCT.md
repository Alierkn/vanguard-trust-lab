# Code of Conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, argue from evidence, distinguish criticism of an idea from criticism of a person, and protect the safety and privacy of others.

Constructive behavior includes stating uncertainty, correcting mistakes openly, respecting different experience levels, and avoiding sensational claims about security products or users.

Harassment, discriminatory language, disclosure of private information, promotion of cheating or bypass activity, and unnecessarily operational exploit content are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub channel available to the repository owner. Do not use a public issue for sensitive personal information.
